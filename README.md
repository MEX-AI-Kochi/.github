## About us

**MEXai** is the AI and software arm of [MEX Automation](https://www.mexautomation.in/), based in Kochi, India. We bring artificial intelligence out of the lab and onto the shop floor, building tools that help industrial teams run safer, leaner and smarter operations.

We believe AI in industry should be **practical**: grounded in real process data, built to work alongside existing automation systems, and designed for the people who operate them every day.

## What we work on

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Plants** | Connecting machines, PLCs and sensors to data pipelines that turn raw signals into insight |
| **Processes** | Monitoring, anomaly detection, predictive maintenance and process optimisation |
| **People** | Dashboards, assistants and tools that put the right information in operators' hands |

## Our principles

- **Built for the floor, not just the demo.** Solutions are designed for real industrial conditions, constraints and uptime requirements.
- **Integrate, don't replace.** We work with the automation infrastructure our clients already have.
- **Explainable by default.** Operators and engineers should understand why a system makes a recommendation.
- **Secure and reliable.** Industrial data is critical data, and we treat it that way.
